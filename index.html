Alisa Liu

Hello! I am a researcher at OpenAI on the pretraining team. Previously I completed my PhD at the University of Washington, where I had the immense fortune of being advised by Yejin Choi and Noah Smith. My PhD research aimed to build better algorithms for language models, including for tokenization, data creation, and inference-time adaptation. I was supported by the NSF Graduate Research Fellowship and OpenAI SuperAlignment Fellowship.
